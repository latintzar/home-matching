## Recipient integration acceptance

Empty-account Supabase bootstrap/RLS, AWS tables/indexes, acquired listings, real analysis/model/photo calls, measured commute, voice account/tool import, microphone permission, billing and outbound communication remain individual recipient commissioning tasks. Preparation makes zero model, paid provider, cloud-write or communication calls.

Two historical cloud WPA cache generations remain: composite listing/mode plus fingerprint/TTL, and listing-only direct lookup. The original scorer consumes four neighborhood contributions; the historical twenty-dimension model is broader. Local sample mode uses one explicit authored cache contract. Dashboard/property/recommendation badges now share real deterministic results.

Original owner account bindings and credential literals remain neutralized. Diagnostic routes stay outside the executable route tree.
